Every project needs a place to start.
This site is that place.

This Hello World is not a formality nor a technical requirement. It is simply the beginning of a personal space where I want to leave a record of what I'm building: ideas, projects, and learnings related to software development.

It's not just about showing final results. Much of the value is in the process. In decisions made on the fly, in errors that force rethinking the path, and in changes that appear while developing something real. Documenting that journey is as important as the product delivered at the end.

This blog will function as an open notebook. Here I will write about projects in development, ideas still in testing phase, and technical reflections that arise during daily work. There will be content focused on iOS development, but also space for backend, web, and tools I'm incorporating over time. Some posts will be brief and direct; others will need more context and detail. The format will depend on what makes sense at each moment.

Writing about what I do helps me think more clearly. Putting technical decisions into words and explaining why one path was chosen over another allows me to detect errors before they become bigger problems. At the same time, sharing that process can be useful for other developers who are on a similar path, even if the context isn't exactly the same.
